Living Roots — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Living Roots is a creator tool that helps users prepare and publish their own short-form educational videos to TikTok.

Information we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we may collect basic account information made available through TikTok, such as your TikTok user ID, username, profile information, and permissions granted during authorization.

We may also process video files, captions, publishing settings, and related metadata that you choose to upload or publish through Living Roots.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide the app functionality, including:

  • connecting your TikTok account;
  • preparing video uploads;
  • publishing or uploading videos that you explicitly choose;
  • managing the publishing workflow;
  • improving app reliability and user experience.

Living Roots does not publish content without user action and confirmation.

TikTok API usage

Living Roots uses TikTok APIs only for features authorized by the user. The app requests only the permissions needed to support creator-controlled video publishing.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only when necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, or interact with TikTok APIs as part of the publishing workflow.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal requirements, or support user-requested publishing actions.

Data deletion

You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at: livingroots271@gmail.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: livingroots271@gmail.com.
